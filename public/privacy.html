Privacy Policy for VESTA-11

Effective Date: March 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to VESTA-11. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our application.

2. Information We Collect

When you log in using Google OAuth, we collect the following information:

  • Your name and profile picture
  • Your email address
  • Your Google User ID

We also collect data related to your usage of the VESTA-11 application, including messages, tasks, and interaction logs within your shared vessel.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect or receive to:

  • Facilitate account creation and logon process.
  • Provide and deliver the services you request.
  • Manage user accounts and maintain the security of our application.
  • Enable user-to-user communications within your designated vessel.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third parties for their commercial purposes. Your data is strictly isolated to your shared vessel and is only accessible by you and your designated co-pilot.

5. Data Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to help protect your personal information. All data is stored securely using Google Cloud and Firebase infrastructure.
